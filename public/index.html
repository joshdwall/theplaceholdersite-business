Phrase Board

Phrase Board is a Windows overlay that stays on top of the job. Staff keep the phrases they repeat — chart notes, ticket replies, handoffs — on a board and copy them in one click.

What we put in place

We watch the real sequence — who waits, who retypes, who forwards — and replace that path with a step the system does. The staff member stays in the job. The busywork leaves.

Handoffs fail when each desk keeps its own version of the work. We put one shared path between them so a request does not die in an inbox.

If the tool adds a login, a new screen, and a second copy of the chart, people go around it. We fit the fix to the screen they already have open.

The analysis happens while the work is happening. We learn the exceptions from the people who hit them, then encode the path they already trust.

The same desk can finish more of the work that gets paid. Headcount stays on the work that needs a person. Repeat steps do not.
